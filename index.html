About Me

I recently graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology. I am interested in information retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, and reasoning and reliability in language models.

I am a remote research assistant at LS3 Lab, University of Toronto & Reviewerly, advised by Prof. Ebrahim Bagheri and Dr. Negar Arabzadeh. Previously, I was a summer research intern at the University of Hong Kong’s STAR Lab, advised by Prof. Reynold Cheng.

My current work focuses on deep research agents: how to help agents find, recognize, and use the evidence needed for well-supported answers. I am investigating question decomposition and evidence retrieval as initial steps toward more reliable research agents.

Research Interests

  • Information retrieval and retrieval-augmented generation
  • Natural language processing and large language models
  • Reasoning and planning in language models
  • LLM reliability and safety
